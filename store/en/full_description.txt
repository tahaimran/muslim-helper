Coming back to Allah starts with one small step.

Maybe you drifted away for a while. Maybe you are a new Muslim and don't know where to begin. Maybe you simply want to be a better Muslim than you were yesterday. Awdah (عودة, "the return") is a quiet companion for that journey: no pressure, no judgment, one day at a time.

WHAT YOU GET
• A 30-day guided path. Each day brings one short lesson, one small action and a moment to reflect.
• Learn salah step by step: wudu, each position, and what to recite, with Arabic, transliteration and meaning.
• Prayer times that work offline. Pick your city from the built-in list or use your phone's location; times are calculated on your phone, no internet needed.
• A simple salah tracker: tap a prayer on the Today screen to mark it as prayed. No streaks to break, no guilt.
• A tasbih and dhikr counter, plus morning and evening adhkar.
• A library of duas for everyday moments.
• Hadith you can check. Every hadith shows where it comes from (Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi, Nasa'i, Ibn Majah) and its grade.
• A reflection journal with a gentle "How is your heart today?" mood check-in.
• Quiet milestones that mark how far you have come.
• Gentle reminders: a daily nudge and prayer-time alerts, off unless you turn them on.
• Backups as a file you keep yourself.
• English, العربية, اردو, Bahasa Indonesia and Türkçe, in light or dark.

It is not another Islamic planner full of boxes to tick. Think of it as a friend who walks beside you while you find your feet again.

WHAT YOU WON'T GET
• No ads.
• No account or sign-up.
• No tracking or analytics.
• No subscriptions or paid upgrades.
Awdah is free forever, and nothing leaves your phone. Your prayers, journal and moods stay with you.

A SINCERE NOTE
Awdah is made as sadaqah jariyah, hoping for reward from Allah alone. The content is drawn from the Quran, Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim and other authentic collections, and every text carries its reference so you can look it up yourself. Where the schools of fiqh differ, Awdah says so gently instead of picking a side.

We are human and we will make mistakes. If something looks wrong, or you have an idea that would help someone coming back, please share your feedback in a review. We read every one.

May Allah make your return easy and accept it from you.
