# Awdah content review checklist

**Purpose.** Awdah is a 30-day return-to-practice app. Every religious item in it (hadith, Quran reading, sunnah, du'a, dhikr, Learn Wudu and Salah content, milestone texts) is listed here so a qualified reviewer (an imam or student of knowledge) can confirm the text, the reference, the grade and the English meaning. Nothing should be treated as vetted religious guidance until it has been reviewed.

**How to mark.** Change `[ ]` to `[x]` if the item is fine, or to `[!]` if there is a problem, and add a short note after the line saying what is wrong and the correction you would make.

**Line format.** `location | first 60 characters of the English meaning | source reference | grade | confidence`. Confidence comes from the verification notes in docs/content-sources: **H** = text and number were checked; **M** = the number was recalled or taken from a secondary source, the grading is disputed among scholars, or the grade was deliberately kept at hasan. A dash means the item has no source (a general or personal du'a).

**Feedback.** Send notes to nafs.app.support@gmail.com.

**Translations.** Translations into other languages are not covered here. They get their own checklist later (Plan 6).

Totals: 241 items listed (dhikr repeated across days are merged), 47 with confidence M, 19 with no source, 22 citations inside free text.
