# Awdah Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-12

Awdah does not collect any data about you.

## Stored on your device

Everything you enter, such as your profile, prayers, tasks and journal, is stored only on your phone (in the app's local storage). We never receive it and we have no server to send it to.

## No accounts, analytics or ads

There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no trackers. Awdah makes no network requests of its own. The only time it connects to the internet is when you tap an external link (for example to quran.com or sunnah.com), which opens in your browser and is governed by that website's own policy.

## Location

If you choose to set your location, it is used only on your phone to calculate prayer times. It is never sent anywhere, and it is not looked up against any address or place-name service. You can also pick a city from a list instead.

## Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone. No push service or server is involved.

## Backups

Backups are files you create and keep. You decide where they go; Awdah never uploads them.

## Deleting your data

You can erase everything in Settings, and uninstalling Awdah deletes all of its data from your phone.

## Children

Awdah is suitable for all ages. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
